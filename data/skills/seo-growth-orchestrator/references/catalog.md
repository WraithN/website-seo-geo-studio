# Specialist routing catalog

## Strategy and diagnosis

- `seo-plan`: build a prioritized SEO roadmap.
- `seo-audit`: run a complete static audit.
- `seo-site-audit-pro`: combine technical, content and live-data checks.
- `seo-coach`: explain workflows and choose a next step for beginners.
- `seo-project-setup`: create durable project context and data intake.
- `competitive-landscape`: map market leaders and strategic gaps.
- `competitor-analysis`: deep-dive one competitor.
- `competitors`: compare social, website and SEO positioning.
- `seo-competitor-pages`: identify competitor pages attracting organic traffic.
- `audit`: judge whether social-search acquisition is worth pursuing.
- `strategy`: build a social-search growth strategy.

## Keywords and demand

- `keyword-research`: discover and prioritize keyword opportunities.
- `keywords`: find high-intent short-video search terms.
- `keyword-clustering`: group terms by intent and target page.
- `seo-serp`: inspect search-result intent and competition.
- `seo-content-brief`: turn evidence into an executable page brief.
- `link-prospecting`: identify realistic backlink and mention opportunities.

## Content and GEO

- `seo-content`: audit and improve content quality.
- `seo-ai-content-check`: detect weak or formulaic AI content patterns.
- `seo-ai-optimization`: improve content for AI-assisted search.
- `seo-geo`: optimize answer-engine and generative-search visibility.
- `seo-eeat-audit`: evaluate experience, expertise, authority and trust.
- `seo-helpful-content`: check whether a page genuinely satisfies users.
- `seo-page`: optimize one page around its intent.
- `seo-programmatic`: plan scalable, data-backed page systems.
- `seo-brand-radar`: monitor brand and competitor search presence.
- `seo-llms-txt`: prepare an llms.txt file.
- `seo-robots-ai`: manage AI crawler directives.
- `seo-markdown-audit`: improve machine-readable Markdown content.

## Technical and on-page

- `seo-technical`: inspect crawlability, indexability and architecture.
- `seo-lighthouse-audit`: analyze Lighthouse findings.
- `seo-core-web-vitals`: diagnose LCP, INP and CLS.
- `seo-page-experience`: review user-facing page experience.
- `seo-images`: improve image performance and discoverability.
- `seo-internal-links`: repair internal-link structure.
- `seo-schema`: build and validate structured data.
- `seo-rich-results`: check rich-result eligibility.
- `seo-sitemap`: audit or create XML sitemaps.
- `seo-hreflang`: validate multilingual targeting.
- `seo-local`: optimize local-search acquisition.
- `seo-log-analysis`: interpret crawler access logs.
- `seo-migration-check`: reduce traffic loss during a site migration.

## Search Console and reporting

- `seo-gsc-overview`: summarize clicks, impressions, CTR and position.
- `seo-gsc-opportunities`: find high-impression or near-page-one opportunities.
- `seo-gsc-new-keywords`: discover newly appearing queries.
- `seo-gsc-content-decay`: locate declining pages.
- `seo-gsc-cannibalization`: detect multiple pages competing for one query.
- `seo-gsc-drops`: investigate sudden losses.
- `seo-gsc-indexing`: inspect indexing evidence.
- `seo-gsc-compare`: compare periods.
- `seo-gsc-brand-vs-nonbrand`: separate brand and acquisition demand.
- `seo-report`: create an executive growth report.
